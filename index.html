The workflow

Import → Generate → Optimize → Publish

The same four steps whether you're listing one product or migrating an entire catalog.

1

Import

Bring in a product photo, a title, or a full CSV/Excel sheet and image folder for bulk runs.

2

Generate

AI writes the title, description, tags, SEO keywords and alt text — one item or thousands, concurrently.

3

Optimize

Review results in the queue, regenerate individual fields, retry failures, and check SEO scores in Analytics.

4

Publish

Export to file or publish directly to your connected marketplace accounts from Publishing Center.
